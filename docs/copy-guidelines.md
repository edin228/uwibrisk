# Copy guidelines (owner's content rules for the redesign)

These rules apply to all new and migrated copy on uwibrisk.com.

## 1. Describe what we do, never promise savings

- Say what UWIB does: we review coverage, compare carriers, explain options, follow up with carriers, manage claims with you.
- Do **not** promise savings or lower costs as an outcome. Avoid phrases such as:
  - "save X%", "save money", "savings of …"
  - "premium credit up to X%"
  - "lower your premiums", "cut costs", "reduce your costs"
  - "reduce reserves", "lower your Ex-Mod" as a promised result
- Process language is fine: "we review reserves", "we follow up with carriers", "we audit carrier logic", "we look for gaps".

A review list of existing copy that may break this rule is generated in
`docs/migration-report.md` under **Copy to revise (cost promises)**.

## 2. Testimonials are verbatim

- Quote testimonials exactly as given. Do not edit wording, grammar or punctuation.
- If a testimonial is shortened, mark every trim with an ellipsis (…).

## 3. No invented facts

- Office hours, response times ("answered within one business day"), counts ("20+ carriers", "50 years"), specialties, survey or form lengths ("takes 5 minutes") and similar claims must come from real data or be confirmed by the owner before publishing.
- When in doubt, leave the claim out and ask.
- Don't promise that a specific person will always be your contact ("the same people at every renewal"). Staff change; promise a team that knows your account instead.

## 4. Photography

- Use black-and-white photography everywhere.
- Exception: employee portraits stay in color.
